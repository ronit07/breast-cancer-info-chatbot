## Lifetime risk
About 12.9% of women born in the U.S. today will develop breast cancer at some point, roughly 1 in 8. For men the lifetime risk is about 0.13%, roughly 1 in 800. Lifetime risk for women born in the 1970s was just under 10%.

## Risk over the next 10 years by starting age
Age 30: 0.49% (about 1 in 204). Age 40: 1.55% (about 1 in 65). Age 50: 2.40% (about 1 in 42). Age 60: 3.54% (about 1 in 28). Age 70: 4.09% (about 1 in 24). These are population averages; an individual's risk depends on many factors, and health professionals can use the Breast Cancer Risk Assessment Tool to estimate it.
